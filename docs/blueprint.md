# **App Name**: SwiftCart

## Core Features:

- Landing Page: Landing page with product choices.
- Product Configuration: Product configuration pages based on selected item from landing page.
- Product Preview: Optional product preview page to review configuration.
- Shopping Basket: Shopping basket to hold configured items.
- PayPal Checkout: Integration with PayPal for payments via PayPal, credit, and debit cards.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Gentle rose (#E0BBE4) for a comforting shopping experience. Derived from the concept of 'innocence' in the prompt, and rose-like softness. In HSL: H300, S40, L75.
- Background color: Lightest rose (#F8F4FA). In HSL: H300, S15, L97. It is subtly related in hue to the primary color but allows it to pop due to low saturation and high brightness.
- Accent color: Mellow violet (#957DAD). In HSL: H270, S30, L60. As a color it is roughly analogous to the primary color, on the bluer side of the rose tone, contrasting in saturation.
- Clean, readable font.
- Simple and intuitive layout for easy navigation.
- Minimalist icons for product options and checkout process.
